Display rules

  1. Times are shown in Singapore time (SGT, UTC+8), for example Tue 6 Oct · 14:00–14:30 SGT.
  2. Status labels: pending is shown as Awaiting agent, confirmed as Confirmed, cancelled as Cancelled, and completed as Completed.
  3. Prices under S$1,000,000 are shown in full, for example S$698,000. Prices of S$1,000,000 or more are shown in millions, rounded to 2 decimal places, for example S$1.25M.
  4. A field that is null is shown as Not provided.
  5. Floor area is shown as 990 sqft (92 sqm), with sqm rounded to a whole number.
  6. My viewings lists every viewing from every page, sorted by start time.
